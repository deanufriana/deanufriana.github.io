# Anti-Slop UI Audit — 001 (2026-11-22)

Audited against `antislop` (R-01 to R-40) and `antislop-ui`. No source files were modified.

## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-37 — Missing `DESIGN.md` / explicit design direction** (`DESIGN.md` absent)
   - No written identity, palette rationale, or `ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION` dials exist, leaving the site on safe AI-portfolio defaults (Inter, `#09090b` dark base, emerald-to-blue gradients, glass cards).

2. **R-39 & R-32 — Clickable `<div>` cards in Work History** (`src/containers/Experience.vue:L115-L124`)
   - Company cards use `<div @click="toggleExpand(work.name)">` with `cursor-pointer`, which cannot be focused with `Tab` or toggled with `Enter`/`Space` and violates native `<button>` semantics.

3. **R-02 — Em dash (`—`) in UI copy and date ranges** (`src/i18n/ui.ts:L64, L69, L126, L131`, `src/containers/Experience.vue:L146, L230`, `src/components/PortfolioPage.astro:L21`)
   - Copy strings (`"intellectual property—your project"`, `"full handover—so your project"`) and date ranges use `—` instead of commas, periods, hyphens, or `"to"`.

4. **R-03 — Interactive tap targets below 44px on mobile** (`src/containers/Navbar.vue`, `src/containers/Projects.vue`, `src/containers/Footer.vue`)
   - Mobile navbar icon buttons (`p-2`), project filter pills (`py-1.5`), and footer social links (`p-2.5`) are under the 44px minimum touch target.

### MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

5. **R-10 — Excessive glassmorphism across navbar and every card** (`src/styles/global.css:L267-L285`, `src/containers/Navbar.vue`, `src/containers/Services.vue`, `src/containers/Experience.vue`, `src/containers/Projects.vue`)
   - `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` is applied simultaneously to the fixed navbar and all `.glass-card` containers across three sections, exceeding the 1–2 element dose cap.

6. **R-01 — Default emerald-to-blue gradients and blurred hero radial orbs** (`src/containers/HomePage.vue:L38-L41, L90`, `src/containers/About.vue:L51`, `src/containers/CallToAction.vue:L27`, `src/pages/404.astro:L14-L21`)
   - Two `blur-3xl` radial orbs sit behind the hero and `from-emerald-400 to-blue-500` gradient text repeats across 4 headings without a brand identity reason.

7. **R-09 & R-19 — Decorative pulsing status dot and endless glow loop** (`src/containers/HomePage.vue:L63-L73`, `src/styles/global.css:L307-L309`)
   - The `"Accepting Freelance Clients"` pill stacks capsule + thin border + endless `badge-pulse` + pulsing `animate-pulse` dot with `ring-4` glow.

8. **R-12 & R-13 — Heavy drop shadows and glows across cards and buttons** (`src/styles/global.css:L283, L299`, `src/components/ui/button/index.ts:L19, L26`)
   - Every `.glass-card` carries `--glass-shadow` and CTA/Upwork buttons carry `shadow-xl`, flattening elevation hierarchy.

9. **R-14 & R-22 — Uniform copy-paste cards and fake browser-chrome previews** (`src/containers/Services.vue:L57-L108`, `src/containers/Projects.vue:L125-L162`)
   - All 4 service cards have identical layout/weight, and all 3 project cards use a synthetic macOS traffic-light browser frame instead of real screenshots or typographic/editorial hierarchy.

10. **R-06 & R-04 — Default `Inter` font, wide-tracked uppercase eyebrows, and uniform Lucide icons** (`src/styles/global.css:L4-L10`, `src/containers/Services.vue`, `src/containers/About.vue`, `src/containers/Experience.vue`, `src/containers/Projects.vue`)
    - Uses `Inter` alone (on the AI default roster), repeats `text-xs font-semibold tracking-widest uppercase` eyebrows above every `h2`, and uses `lucide-vue-next` everywhere without a written rationale.

### LOW (Quality Locks)

11. **R-05 — Uniform section rhythm and alternating background flips** (`src/containers/Services.vue`, `src/containers/Experience.vue`, `src/containers/Projects.vue`, `src/containers/CallToAction.vue`)
    - Every section shares the same `py-20 sm:py-28` + `max-w-6xl px-6` cadence and alternates `--section-alt-bg` without compositional variation.

12. **R-11 — Overused pill radius (`rounded-full`) across unrelated controls** (`src/containers/HomePage.vue`, `src/containers/About.vue`, `src/containers/Experience.vue`, `src/containers/Projects.vue`)
    - `rounded-full` is used on CTAs, status badges, skill tags, role badges, and filter buttons alike instead of reserving pill shapes for one deliberate tier.

13. **R-21 — Hardcoded dark mode default ignoring OS preference** (`src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro:L38, L157-L165`, `src/containers/Navbar.vue:L20-L32`)
    - Defaults to `<html class="dark">` without checking `window.matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)')` when `localStorage.theme` is unset.

## Follow-up Resolution (All 13 Items Fixed)

- **#1 (R-37):** Created `DESIGN.md` defining the **Precision Editorial Engineering** direction (`ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2`), neutral paper/charcoal palette, single emerald accent, editorial serif display pairing, and surface/radius rules.
- **#2 (R-39, R-32):** Replaced clickable `<div>` cards in `src/containers/Experience.vue` with native `<button type="button" :aria-expanded="...">` triggers while keeping company `<a href>` links independent.
- **#3 (R-02):** Removed all em dashes (`—`) from `src/i18n/ui.ts`, `src/containers/Experience.vue`, `src/components/PortfolioPage.astro`, and `src/pages/404.astro`.
- **#4 (R-03):** Raised mobile tap targets to `min-h-11 min-w-11` (44px) across `src/containers/Navbar.vue`, `src/containers/Projects.vue`, `src/containers/Footer.vue`, `src/containers/Services.vue`, `src/containers/Experience.vue`, `src/containers/HomePage.vue`, and `src/containers/CallToAction.vue`.
- **#5, #8 (R-10, R-12, R-13):** Restricted `backdrop-filter: blur` solely to `.glass-nav`; replaced `.glass-card` and heavy `shadow-xl` styles with flat `.surface-card` / `.surface-card-interactive` hairline borders in `src/styles/global.css` and `src/components/ui/button/index.ts`.
- **#6, #7 (R-01, R-09, R-19):** Removed hero/404 `blur-3xl` radial orbs, gradient text headings, and the pulsing `badge-pulse` capsule; replaced the availability indicator in `src/containers/HomePage.vue` with a static emerald dot and plain status label.
- **#9, #10, #11, #12 (R-04, R-05, R-06, R-11, R-14, R-22):** Paired `Inter` with italic serif display accents, replaced identical service cards with a numbered `01`–`04` editorial index in `src/containers/Services.vue`, replaced synthetic traffic-light browser frames with an asymmetric featured-first layout in `src/containers/Projects.vue`, varied section rhythm without `--section-alt-bg` flips, and reserved `rounded-full` for primary pill CTAs.
- **#13 (R-21):** Updated `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` and `src/containers/Navbar.vue` to respect `prefers-color-scheme` when `localStorage.theme` is unset.
